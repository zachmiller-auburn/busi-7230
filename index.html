Quick-reference formulas

Revenue variance
Actual Revenue − Budgeted Revenue
Positive = Favorable (F). Negative = Unfavorable (U).
Cost variance (VC or FC)
Actual Cost − Budgeted Cost
Negative = Favorable (F, spent less). Positive = Unfavorable (U, spent more).
Net impact on operating income
ΔOI = ΔRevenue − ΔVC − ΔFC
The minus signs on costs are what flips a favorable cost variance into a positive OI impact.
Contribution margin
Revenue − Variable Costs (pre-fixed-cost profit).
Cash from operations (indirect)
OI + Depreciation − ΔA/R − ΔInventory − ΔPrepaids + ΔA/P
F/U cheat
Favorable always means helps OI. Unfavorable always means hurts OI. The sign of the raw variance flips between revenue and costs — the label does not.
